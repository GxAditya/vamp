# Genre DNA

Each entry is a starting point: tempo, feel, harmonic language, the signature rhythm, a palette of real Vamp GM programs, and the giveaways that make a fake version sound fake. Beat positions are zero-based quarter-note beats inside a 4/4 bar (0, 0.5 ... 3.5).

## Lyrical piano / film-like (Hisaishi, Einaudi-adjacent)

- 66-92 BPM, 4/4 or 3/4. Major with borrowed iv, secondary dominants, suspensions; minor-plagal endings.
- Arpeggiated left hand or Alberti, singing right-hand melody, strings entering for the return, woodwind answers.
- Palette: grand piano 0, string ensemble 48, cello 42, clarinet 71, flute 73, oboe 68, harp 46.
- Fake giveaways: block chords on every beat, melody without rests, no dynamic arc.

## Lo-fi hip-hop

- 70-90 BPM, 8ths swung to +0.6-0.65. Minor 7ths and 9ths, ii-V loops, i9-VImaj7.
- Kick 0, 1.62, 2.5; snare 1, 3; ghost snare 2.87; swung hats at 30-50; open hat at the end of 4 bars.
- Palette: tine EP 4, upright 32 or finger 33, vibraphone 11, jazz guitar 26, rain 96, kit.
- Production: `lowpass` 4500-6000 on everything melodic, reverb 0.2 / 2.4, a little delay on the lead.
- Giveaways: straight 8ths, bright highs, triads instead of 7th chords.

## Neo-soul / R&B

- 68-96 BPM, behind-the-beat feel (hats and snare +0.02-0.04 late). Extended chords (9, 11, 13), ii-V-I, chromatic planing, Dorian minor.
- Kick syncopated (0, 0.75, 2.5), snare 1 and 3 with ghosts, 16th hats with dynamics.
- Palette: tine EP 4, fretless 35 or finger 33, drawbar organ 16 low, alto sax 65 / tenor 66, muted guitar 28 licks.
- Giveaways: rigid quantize, root-position triads, no bass movement.

## House / French house

- 118-126 BPM, four-on-the-floor kick 0, 1, 2, 3; clap 1, 3; open hat on every offbeat.
- Minor 7th loops, filtered (low-pass automation is the signature), syncopated octave bass on the offbeats.
- Palette: synth bass 38, poly pad 90, bright piano 1 stabs on 0.5 and 2.5, electric grand 2, organ 18 stabs.
- Arrangement: 8-16 bar sections, filter build, a 1-bar kick dropout before each drop.
- Giveaways: no filter movement, no breakdown, piano chords on the beat.

## Synthwave / retrowave

- 90-118 BPM. Minor (Aeolian), i - VI - III - VII, octave-pumping 8th-note bass.
- Kick 0, 2; big snare 1, 3 (drum reverb 0.25 / 1.6); 8th hats; tom fills.
- Palette: synth bass 38, poly pad 90, fifths lead 86, saw lead 81 (sparingly), crystal 98 arpeggios.
- Giveaways: no arpeggio motion, dry snare, a melody in the same register as the pad.

## Pop ballad / anthem

- 66-80 BPM (ballad) or 100-124 (anthem). Major, I-V-vi-IV and its rotations, sus2/sus4 colours, a final-chorus modulation up a whole step.
- Verse sparse (piano or guitar 8ths), pre-chorus rising (bass walks up), chorus full (drums, strings, octave-doubled melody).
- Palette: grand 0 / bright 1, steel guitar 25, strings 48, finger bass 33, kit.
- Giveaways: chorus no bigger than the verse, no pre-chorus lift, melody peaking in the verse.

## Cinematic orchestral (heroic / epic)

- 70-100 BPM. Minor with bVI-bVII-i, pedal tones, ostinatos in 8ths/16ths, slow harmonic rhythm.
- Low strings and horns carry the theme; trumpets and choir at the peak; toms and big hits at section changes.
- Palette: string ensemble 48, tremolo 44, cello 42, French horn 60, brass section 61, trumpet 56, choir 52, harp 46, toms 45/47, crash 49.
- Giveaways: every instrument playing the melody, no ostinato, brass sustaining everything.

## Ambient

- 50-72 BPM or no felt pulse. Lydian and major-7 colours, pedal tones, slow chord changes (1 per 2-4 bars).
- Overlapping long notes, sparse bells or kalimba, swells via volume and filter automation.
- Palette: warm pad 89, halo 94, new age 88, bowed glass 92, atmosphere 99, kalimba 108, fretless 35.
- Giveaways: drums, a busy melody, abrupt note endings.

## Jazz (small group, swing / bossa)

- Swing 120-200 BPM (8ths at +0.667) or bossa 120-140 (straight 8ths, syncopated). ii-V-I, tritone substitution, 7th/9th/13th voicings, guide-tone lines.
- Swing: ride 51 pattern 0, 1, 1.667, 2, 3, 3.667; hat 44 on 1 and 3; walking bass quarter notes with approach tones.
- Bossa: rim click 37 on 0, 0.75, 1.5, 2.5, 3.25 (two-bar clave-like pattern), kick 0, 1.5, 2, 3.5.
- Palette: grand 0, upright 32, jazz guitar 26, vibraphone 11, tenor sax 66, trumpet 56, flute 73 (bossa), nylon 24 (bossa).
- Giveaways: triads, straight 8ths in swing, a bass that doesn't walk.

## Folk / acoustic

- 80-120 BPM, 4/4 or 6/8. Diatonic major, I-IV-vi-V, Mixolydian bVII, open voicings with drones.
- Strummed steel 25 or picked nylon 24, fiddle 110, banjo 105, flute 73, acoustic bass 32, tambourine 54, shaker 70.
- Giveaways: perfectly quantized strums, electric bass tone, a drum kit that is too loud.

## Funk / disco

- 100-120 BPM. Dominant 9th and minor 7th vamps on one chord, syncopated 16ths.
- Kick 0, 0.75, 2.5; snare 1, 3; 16th hats; ghost snares; octave bass with slides suggested by short grace notes.
- Palette: clavinet 7, muted guitar 28, finger bass 33 / synth bass 38, brass section 61 stabs, string ensemble 48 (disco), electric grand 2.
- Giveaways: bass on the downbeats only, no 16th-note interplay, long notes everywhere.

## Game: RPG town / overworld

- 100-140 BPM. Bright major, 2- and 4-bar phrases, period structure, a melody you hum after one loop.
- Pizzicato 45, marimba 12, flute 73, clarinet 71, harp 46, acoustic bass 32 or bassoon 70, light tambourine or claves.
- Loop seamlessly: the last bar's harmony leads to bar 1 (V or IV → I), no reverb tail cut; keep loops 30-90 s.
- Giveaways: no counter-melody, a melody that never resolves, a loop seam that jumps.

## Game: battle / boss

- 140-180 BPM. Minor, driving 8th-note ostinato, chromatic bass, bVI-bVII-i, a short heroic motif.
- Kick 8ths or 0, 1.5, 2, 3; snare 1, 3 with rolls into sections; toms; crashes on downbeats.
- Palette: string ensemble 48 / tremolo 44 ostinato, brass section 61, synth bass 38, overdrive guitar 29, choir 52, fifths lead 86.
- Giveaways: no ostinato, slow harmonic rhythm with nothing driving, no motif.

## Chiptune-flavoured (with GM)

- 120-160 BPM. Major or minor, fast arpeggios (16ths over the chord tones, which imply the chord), square-ish leads.
- Fifths lead 86 or saw lead 81 for the melody, synth bass 38 octave patterns, crystal 98 arpeggios, simple kit.
- Giveaways: reverb and pads (chiptune is dry and lean), more than 4 parts at once.

## Classical-style miniature (minuet, waltz, prelude)

- 3/4 for minuets and waltzes (bass on beat 0, chord on 1 and 2), 4/4 preludes with broken chords.
- Periods and sentences, authentic cadences, secondary dominants, a modulation to V (major) or III (minor) in the middle.
- Palette: grand 0 alone, or string ensemble 48 / violin 40 / cello 42 as a quartet, harpsichord 6 for baroque.
- Giveaways: pop progressions, drums, no cadences.
